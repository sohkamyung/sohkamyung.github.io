# "Sinophagia: A Celebration of Chinese Horror 2024" edited by Xueting Christine Ni

[Main Index](../../../README.md) / [Reviews Index](../../README.md) / [Fiction Reviews Index](../README.md) / [2026 Reviews Index](README.md)

*Rating: 4/5 Stars.*

A fabulous collection of horror stories based on contemporary Chinese culture that show that the horrors that can lurk just beneath the surface of modern China. Each story ends with a note by the editor on the story, the author and aspects of the story itself. Unbelievably, some of the situations presented in the story are based on the real-life experiences of people living in China.

Of the stories presented, I enjoyed the ones by Hong Niangzi, Fan Zhou, Chuan Ge, Zhou Dedong, Zhou Haohui and Su Min.

- <a id="the-girl-in-the-rain"></a>"The Girl in the Rain" by Hong Niangzi: at a university, tales are told to beware of red umbrellas that suck your blood and pretty girls in the rain that suck your body dry. So when a girl is offered a red umbrella and a boy sees a girl in the rain, the results seem inevitable. But all is not all it seems in this tale of deception and jealousy.

- <a id="the-waking-dream"></a>"The Waking Dream" by Fan Zhou: a woman who works in a virtual environment sees monsters along with her colleagues. She also has her own monster, and it gradually becomes worse. Then one day, she realises the monstrous visions are a danger signal, but it may already be too late for the person in danger.

- <a id="immortal-beauty"></a>"Immortal Beauty" by Chu Xidao: a girl is married to a lord, but is later cast aside for other brides. When she loses her life in a betrayal, she comes back as a revengeful spirit, and develops a liking for a kind of mortal clothing.

- <a id="those-who-walk-at-night-walk-with-ghosts"></a>"Those Who Walk at Night, Walk with Ghosts" by She Cong Ge: a rural doctor is desperate to get a patient to a hospital and decides to take a mountain route that goes through the site of a bandit massacre. Apparent ghostly manifestations occur, that prey on the minds of the nearby villagers with folk beliefs and a connection to the bandits.

- <a id="the-ying-yang-pot"></a>"The Ying Yang Pot" by Chuan Ge: a man stumbles into a hotpot restaurant that serves as a place where the living can meet the dead. He unknowingly orders a hotpot that allows a ghost to 'latch on' and gradually drain his life. An elderly spiritualist offers a solution, but it is one that will return to haunt him at the end.

- <a id="shaxiao"></a>"Shaxiao" by Goodnight, Xiaoqing: a man encounters a monster in a forest, and a beautiful huntress who hunt the monster. But when he gets close, he discovers the truth behind her and the monsters that she hunts and also controls her.

- <a id="have-you-heard-of-ancient-glory"></a>"Have You Heard of Ancient Glory?" by Zhou Dedong: a man buys a flat in a complex that, despite being popular, is strangely devoid of people. The truth would reveal a social situation where the high cost of dying has lead people to an alternative housing situation for the remains of relatives in China.

- <a id="records-of-xiangxi"></a>"Records of Xiangxi" by Nanpai Sanshu: a tale, told as a record of an event in the past, of a group of fighters who enter heart of a jungle to hunt a terrifying monster. But the group also encounter a form of ancient beliefs about humans who lived in the ground with supernatural abilities.

- <a id="the-ghost-wedding"></a>"The Ghost Wedding" by Yimei Tangguo: the story starts with a wedding in a rural village, but then goes back in time to cover events that lead up to the wedding, involving the kidnapping of a woman, and her daughter who falls in with a village boy and how they are reunited in a wedding that is not your usual type of wedding.

- <a id="night-climb"></a>"Night Climb" by Chi Hui: a man climbs a mountain at night to see the dawn, but encounters people along the way; if they are people.

- <a id="forbidden-rooms"></a>"Forbidden Rooms" by Zhou Haohui: a girl and a man find themselves trapped in a home by someone who wants to punish them over the death of an old man and a young child: the girl did nothing when she heard the child's cries for help, while the man was the child's father. Their attempts to escape depends on the girl making the right choices to free them. But the true horror of their situation is revealed with the final attempt to escape.

- <a id="tinaang"></a>"Ti'Naang" by Su Min: one girl returns to a town that employs "body doubles" made by the villagers themselves. She is there to tell her domineering father about her impending marriage and determined to show that her mother is who she is. But she will discover the real truth that will cause her to re-evaluate her opinions about herself and her family.

- <a id="huangcun"></a>"Huangcun" by Cai Jun: an author is astonished to discover that a fictional village he wrote about exists when a girl approaches him for his autograph. Determined to visit it, he follows the girl back to the village, only to discover a strange story about a flute that can summon ghosts. But in a place where truth and myth have mixed, just who are the ghosts in the story?

- <a id="the-death-of-nala"></a>"The Death of Nala" by Gu Shi: a mother finds herself horrified at her son, who has mistreated a kitten. But it is only when she goes for a mental check-up do we discover the true horror behind the mistreatment.

*Book read from 2026/11/12 to 2026/11/19.*
