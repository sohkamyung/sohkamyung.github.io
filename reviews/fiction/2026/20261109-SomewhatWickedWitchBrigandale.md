# "The Somewhat Wicked Witch of Brigandale" by C.M. Waggoner

[Main Index](../../../README.md) / [Reviews Index](../../README.md) / [Fiction Reviews Index](../README.md) / [2026 Reviews Index](README.md)

*Rating: 3/5 Stars.*

*Book read from 2026/11/09 to 2026/11/10.*

A humorous tale of a witch who finds a baby left on her doorstep and decides to raise him. After reaching manhood, he is revealed as a prince and proclaimed to be the saviour of the kingdom. So, off he goes to reclaim his kingdom from the usurper, which he unexpectedly does, and becomes the king. The repercussions would take up the rest of the book.

One of his first acts as king would, unfortunately, bring misfortune to the kingdom. So it is up to his mother to come along and help to put thing right. In the process of helping his son set up his government and run the kingdom, she realises that he isn't really up to the task of being king and sets out to bring his downfall.

A fun and 'cozy fantasy' story where wicked witches are only somewhat wicked and may curse others or give gifts. And being a witch does not stop this witch from being a mother to a son who had led a mostly protected life growing up, and may not be ready to run a kingdom on his own without help (or hindrance).
