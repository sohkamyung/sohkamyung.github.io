# "Snakes of the World: A Guide to Every Family" by Mark O'Shea

[Main Index](../../../README.md) / [Reviews Index](../../README.md) / [Nonfiction Reviews Index](../README.md) / [2026 Reviews Index](README.md)

*Rating: 4/5 Stars.*

A lovely book about the snakes. Packed full of information, it starts off with an introduction to what is a snake and what differentiates it from other animals and limbless lizards. It then goes into the biology of snakes. All snakes are predators, going for small food items like earthworms to large organisms like alligators and large mammals. Their also inhabit numerous environments from the oceans to mountains and the tropics to the colder regions.

The book then covers the various families of snakes, some of which are well known (like the cobras and pythons) while others are so new to science that even some basic biological facts (like whether they lay eggs or give birth to live young) are still not known. Detailed pictures of the various snakes are given, including some with very striking patterns to those so well camouflaged that you may have trouble finding them out in the field.

A good book to read to get to know the snakes in the world. General readers are free to skip over some of the more technical descriptions about snake anatomy and biology, while admiring some of the more beautiful examples of snakes found in the world.
