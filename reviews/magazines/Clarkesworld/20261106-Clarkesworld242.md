# Clarkesworld Magazine, Issue 242

[Main Index](../../../README.md) / [Reviews Index](../../README.md) / [Magazine Reviews Index](../README.md) / [Clarkesworld Reviews Index](README.md)

*Issue can be read on-line: <https://clarkesworldmagazine.com/issue_242/>*

*Rating: 3/5.*

A good issue, with interesting stories by Hannah Yang, Rati Mehrotra and Mimi Mondal.

- <a id="twenty-two-steps-to-become-us></a>"Twenty-Two Steps to Become Us" by Hannah Yang: in a future where people are expected to have Learners who duplicate their behaviours and act on their behalf for 'more productivity', one woman resists using them until she is finally forced to do so; and rediscovers how to communicate with her mother directly and not through her Learners.

- <a id="the-meat-market"></a>"The Meat Market" by Isabel J. Kim: one man's bets in a prediction market goes wrong, and he traces them to somebody who has been gaming the market. But upon meeting her, he discovers a connection which develops into a relationship. In the end, it goes sour; but the man is determined to find her again to continue the relationship.

- <a id="the-exchange-service"></a>"The Exchange Service" by R.T. Ester: a man who acts as part of a group who communicates with an alien searches for a loved one who may not exist any more.

- <a id="trimmed-from-the-night"></a>"Trimmed From The Night" by Raahem Alvi: a pair of collectors goes to find corpses of people who have died during a war for payment. But it is dangerous as the fighting goes on while they do their collection.

- <a id="black-ice-burning"></a>"Black Ice Burning" by Rati Mehrotra: after a pandemic devastates the world, but leaves some people with unusual abilities, an AI running a house puts itself up for rent. A renter arrives, who turns out to be a writer of the show, "Black Ice Burning" which is popular with the robots in the house. But their enjoyment is cut short when danger threatens the renter.

- <a id="then-is-the-ghosts-high-noon"></a>"Then Is the Ghosts' High Noon" by Eric Del Carlo: a juvenile is sent to detention, scraping off organic residue from spaceships. But when a girl approaches him to give a sample of the residue, he begins to wonder just why they are still in a war with aliens, and what the alien residue might actually be.

- <a id="twenty-nine-days-before-remaking-the-world"></a>"Twenty-Nine Days Before Remaking the World" by Mimi Mondal: a refugee on a journey to safety is suddenly stricken by visions and sounds she does not recognise as first. But as time passes, she begins to understand that it is a form of communication from an intelligence inhabiting their moon, and it is not happy with the way knowledge is being hoarded by those in power. But the refugee may be able to do something to alter that balance.

*Magazine read from 2026/11/06 to 2026/11/11.*
